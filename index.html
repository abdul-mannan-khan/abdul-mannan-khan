01

About

I am a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in Electronics and Robotics at the University of West London, where I lead five modules across Levels 3 to 7, act as a Personal Academic Tutor, and supervise a PhD candidate. I am a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy and hold a Postgraduate Certificate in Academic Practice (2025). Before joining the University of West London, I was a Research Associate in Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence for Autonomous Systems at Imperial College London and a Senior Research Associate at the Bristol Robotics Laboratory, University of Bristol.

I have more than twelve years of university teaching experience across Pakistan, South Korea and the United Kingdom. As an Assistant Professor at the University of Engineering and Technology Taxila, I secured the National Research Program for Universities grant, a first within the faculty, and was the main editor of the Self-Assessment Report that earned the department's degree Washington Accord recognition. I also launched the M.Sc. Mechatronics Engineering programme at the Chakwal sub-campus and managed final year projects for the B.Sc. programme.

I have taught at the University of Engineering and Technology Taxila, Hanyang University and Hanbat National University in South Korea, and at Brunel University London, Imperial College London and the University of West London in the UK. I have also mentored students at these universities and at the University of Bristol. I hold UKRI Global Talent endorsement.

02

Research

My research combines robotics, control and artificial intelligence, backed by a strong mathematical and programming background in embedded systems, ROS, C/C++ and Python.

Trustworthy & Explainable Artificial Intelligence

Explainable reinforcement learning and causal learning for safe autonomous systems and 6G networks.

Drones & Autonomous Systems

Large language model drone navigation, drone relay positioning, multi-drone tracking and deep reinforcement learning for quadcopters.

Soft Robotics & Shape Memory Alloy Actuators

Modelling and neural-network control of shape memory alloy actuators, robotic hands and caterpillar robots.

Rehabilitation Robotics

Upper-limb exoskeletons, rehabilitation gloves and human motion-intention estimation.

Nonlinear & Adaptive Control

Sliding mode, backstepping, impedance and adaptive control for robots and vehicles.

Engineering Education

Sensors and actuators, automation and control, and project-based learning in robotics and artificial intelligence.
