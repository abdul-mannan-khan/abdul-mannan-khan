About Me

I am a Lecturer in Electronics and Robotics at the University of West London, where I lead five modules across Levels 3 to 7 (Sensors and Actuators, Automation and Control, Applied Engineering Project, Essential Technology, and Control Systems Theory and Practice), act as a Personal Academic Tutor, and supervise a PhD candidate. I am a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (FHEA) and hold a Postgraduate Certificate in Academic Practice (2025). Before joining UWL, I was a Research Associate in Trustworthy AI for Autonomous Systems at Imperial College London and a Senior Research Associate at the Bristol Robotics Laboratory, University of Bristol.

I possess extensive multinational teaching experience across Pakistan, South Korea, and the United Kingdom. In my role as an Assistant Professor at the University of Engineering and Technology in Taxila, Pakistan, I achieved notable success in research funding, securing the prestigious National Research Program for Universities grant – a unique accomplishment within the faculty. My efforts were instrumental in obtaining accreditation for the department's degree from the Washington Accord, enabling recognition in the USA for the first time. I played a crucial role as the main editor of the Self-Assessment Report and was pivotal in the development of the Continuous Quality Improvement loop. Additionally, I spearheaded the successful initiation of the M.Sc. Mechatronics Engineering program at UET Taxila's Chakwal Sub-Campus and managed the Final Year Projects for the B.Sc. Mechatronics Engineering program.

I have taught various subjects at the University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila, Pakistan, Hanyang University and Hanbat National University in South Korea, and Brunel University London, Imperial College London and the University of West London in the UK. Additionally, I have mentored students at these universities, as well as at the University of Bristol, UK, guiding them to produce high-quality research.

Research Summary

I have expertise in robotics, control and artificial intelligence, backed by a strong mathematical and programming background. I have authored 42 publications, including 26 Q1/Q2 journal articles, with over 720 citations and an h-index of 15. My recent work focuses on trustworthy and explainable AI for autonomous systems, deep reinforcement learning, large language models for drone navigation, and UAV networks for 6G. My earlier research spans human-robot interaction, rehabilitation and assistive exoskeletons, soft robotics and shape memory alloy (SMA) actuators, manipulators, mobile robots and drones. This work builds on my expertise in embedded systems, ROS and programming languages including C/C++ and Python. My academic qualifications include a Ph.D. in Mechanical Design Engineering. I hold UKRI Global Talent endorsement.
